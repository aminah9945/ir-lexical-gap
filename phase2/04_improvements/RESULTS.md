## E3: cross-encoder re-ranking (CPU sample; `results/e3_ce_*.csv`, `figures/e3_ce_bars.png`)

Setup:
- Model: cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2, max_length 512.
- Sample: ≤ 150 queries per dataset (seed 42); all queries for TREC-COVID (50) and Touché (49); 150 of Quora's 10k.
- 131k pairs scored at ~22-28 pairs/s (4 CPU threads).

Results:
- **CE(BM25 top-100) − BM25: +0.025 macro over 8 datasets** (p = 0.004; Holm +4/−1).
  - TREC-COVID +0.143, FiQA +0.101, NFCorpus +0.031, SciDocs +0.025.
  - Touché −0.164 [−0.230, −0.100].
  - ArguAna 0.000.
- CE(BM25) − H(BGE-s): −0.059 (5/8 Holm-significant negative). The fixed hybrid of two small bi-encoder systems beats BM25 + CE.
- CE(H(MiniLM) top-100) − H(MiniLM): −0.040 (DL −0.030 [−0.053, −0.006]). ArguAna is −0.120 (Holm p < 1e-3).

## E4: why routing fails (`e4_why_routing_fails.py`, `figures/e4_indomain_bars.png`, `figures/e4_fewshot.png`)

**In-domain ceiling** (macro):

| Config | always-H | in-domain best fixed | in-domain router | dataset oracle | query oracle | router − fixed (p) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A_bge_std_k8 | 0.534 | 0.551 | 0.551 | 0.553 | 0.598 | −0.0005 (0.66) |
| A_minilm_std_k8 | 0.514 | 0.528 | 0.524 | 0.528 | 0.576 | −0.0036 (0.99) |
| B_minilm_std_k5 | 0.436 | 0.440 | 0.438 | 0.440 | 0.494 | −0.0016 (0.85) |

- The only dataset with a significant query-level gain beyond the prior is Quora (8k in-domain training queries): +0.0046, Holm p = 0.0008.

**Split-half dataset oracle.** +0.0182 macro over H for BGE-s (in-sample +0.0188); MiniLM +0.0130 (in-sample +0.0134).

**Few-shot selection (exploratory, post-hoc).** Choose the fixed system for a dataset with k labelled queries:

| k | BGE-s | MiniLM |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | +0.004 | −0.004 |
| 10 | +0.010 | +0.005 |
| 20 | +0.014 | +0.009 |
| 30 | +0.016 | +0.010 |

**Headroom anatomy.**
- Queries with oracle gain > 0.2 are 2-27% of queries but carry 23-87% of the headroom.
- On 1-relevant-document datasets (ArguAna, SciFact, Quora), queries with ≤ 2 relevant documents carry 83-100% of the headroom.
- Ties (|Δ_B| ≤ 0.01) are 41-72% of queries outside TREC-COVID/Touché.
