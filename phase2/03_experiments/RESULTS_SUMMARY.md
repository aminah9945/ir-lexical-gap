**Per dataset** (Tier A BGE-s, selected):
- ArguAna: +0.048, significant (the router sends 91% of queries to D).
- SciDocs: −0.004, significant.
- Touche: −0.082, significant. 0% of queries are routed to BM25 because no training domain has BM25 ≫ dense. This is the hardest-transfer fold.

**Why routing fails.**
- Within held-out datasets, the Kendall τ between predicted and true Δ_B (BM25 − H) is only 0.03-0.11 (Tier A) and 0.12 (Tier B). For Δ_D it is about 0.
- The router beats random routing with the same mix by only 0-0.008.
- The pre-retrieval QPP predictors correlate with dense nDCG as strongly as with BM25 nDCG (Sec. 4). They measure difficulty, not lexical suitability.

**Framing-A evidence that survives.**
- The query-level oracle headroom over always-H, +0.064 (BGE-s), is 3.4× the dataset-level headroom (+0.019).
- Per-query winner shares (`results/fig1_winshare.csv`, `figures/fig1_winshare_*.png`): BM25 is the strict winner (>0.01) on 3-24% of queries everywhere, and on 55-65% on Touche.
- So "the query decides" holds for the oracle, but qrel-free query-time features cannot exploit it across domains. **Always-hybrid is the robust default.**

**Efficiency router (Tier A, B vs H).**
- The share-vs-nDCG curve lies on the random-assignment line (`figures/w1_efficiency_A_*.png`).
- At the operating point pre-declared on the training folds, it serves 5.7-7.9% of queries BM25-only, at a held-out macro loss of 0.004-0.008.

## 3. W3: gap model (dense − BM25 vs cov_mean; explanatory, uses qrels)

**Model B (primary).** Per-dataset OLS with HC3 errors, then DL pooling of the z(cov_mean) slope, with controls log(q_terms), z(idf) and log(n_rel).

| D | Pooled slope [95% CI] | p | I² | Negative / significant | LODO range | Without small datasets |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MiniLM | −0.059 [−0.099, −0.019] | 0.004 | 0.97 | 8/8, 5 sig. | −0.072 to −0.052 | −0.064 [−0.108, −0.019] |
| BGE-s | −0.063 [−0.109, −0.016] | 0.008 | 0.98 | 7/8, 5 sig. | −0.073 to −0.054 | −0.055 [−0.108, −0.002] |

**Model A** (MixedLM with random intercept and random slope; converged): MiniLM −0.065 [−0.091, −0.039]; BGE-s −0.061 [−0.090, −0.031].

**Circularity (C3).**
- Pooled coverage slope on nDCG_B is 0.182 [0.144, 0.219]. On nDCG_D it is 0.120 (MiniLM) and 0.119 (BGE-s).
- slope_B − slope_D = +0.059 [0.046, 0.073] (MiniLM) and +0.063 [0.050, 0.076] (BGE-s), with a stratified bootstrap.
- The ratio D/B ≈ 0.66: about two thirds of the overlap-effectiveness link is generic difficulty, and one third is lexical-specific. **C3 is supported.**
- Note that slope_B − slope_D ≡ −gap slope by OLS linearity.

**Multinomial robustness model.** One SD more coverage gives OR 0.51 for "dense wins vs BM25 wins" (both models, p<1e-4).

**W2 (qrel-free proxies, MiniLM, 5 datasets).**
- ρ(pcov_D@10, cov_mean) = 0.39-0.53. ρ(pcov_B@10, cov_mean) = 0.10-0.39, which is near-tautological.
- With the proxy in place of cov_mean, the gap slope vanishes: pcov_D10 gives −0.006 [−0.022, 0.011], and pcov_gap gives +0.003 [−0.023, 0.029].
- **The deployable version of H5 does not hold.** This agrees with the router result.

## 7. Pending on Colab (W4 and the 8-dataset Tier B) and how to include it

The student runs `phase2_dense_runs.ipynb` (Drive 01_notebooks) and unzips `phase2_dense_runs.zip` into `phase2/03_experiments/colab_runs/`. That folder should then contain `run_<minilm|bge|bgebase>_<ds>.npz` and `perquery_*.csv`. Then run:

```
cd /home/user/ir_lexical_gap && nohup bash phase2/03_experiments/run_all.sh > phase2/03_experiments/logs/run_all.log 2>&1 &
```

Runtime is about 60-80 min. The W6 grid is cached; the routers take about 4.5 min per config, 3 in parallel.

This automatically:
1. Checks the sanity gates (`results/colab_sanity.csv`):
   - MiniLM per-query nDCG within 1e-3 of Phase 1;
   - BGE-s macro within 0.002.
   The pipeline stops if a gate fails.
2. Builds H(BGE-b) labels from the BM25 run and the bge-base run.
3. Computes Tier B features for MiniLM on Quora/TREC-COVID/Touche, and for BGE-s and BGE-b on all 8.
4. Adds BGE-b to W7, W3 and the QPP evaluation.
5. Runs the routers:
   - Tier A for BGE-b;
   - Tier B on 8 datasets for all three D;
   - BM25-LODO rows for every D with rankings.
6. Adds dense judged@k on all 8 datasets to W9.

## 8. Agent 4 improvement experiments (added 2026-10-22)

See `../04_improvements/RESULTS.md`, and `../04_interpretation.md` for the pre-declaration and the thesis.

**No deployable improvement over always-hybrid** (family F4, Holm):
- P1, per-query fusion α: −0.007.
- P2, label-free CE-teacher α: −0.004.
- P3, CE re-ranking of the hybrid: −0.040.

**Key diagnostic.** An in-domain (5-fold CV) router equals the in-domain best fixed system: +0.017 over always-H, but −0.0005 vs fixed. Query-time QPP features therefore carry no exploitable query-level preference signal even in-domain; Quora (8k training queries, +0.005) is the only exception. Dataset-level selection with 10-30 labelled queries gives +0.010 to +0.016 (exploratory).
